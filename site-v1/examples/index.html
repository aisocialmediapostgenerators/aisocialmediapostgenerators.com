LinkedIn

We improved onboarding by deleting, not adding.

Two screens looked helpful in the flow. The data said otherwise.

New users were pausing before they reached the first useful outcome, so we removed both screens and moved the essential guidance into context.

Time-to-value dropped 40%. The lesson: onboarding is not a tour of your product. It is the shortest credible path to value.
