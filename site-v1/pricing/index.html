Pricing questions

What counts as one generation?

One request creates three distinct editable drafts and uses one generation credit.

Do unused monthly credits roll over?

No. Monthly credits reset at the beginning of each billing period.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. A cancellation stops renewal at the end of the current paid period.

Are outputs guaranteed to be accurate?

No. You are responsible for reviewing claims, links, dates and compliance before publishing.
