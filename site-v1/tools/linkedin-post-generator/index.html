How to get a stronger LinkedIn draft

Include one concrete detail: a result, a mistake, a surprising observation or a decision you made. Tell the generator who needs the lesson and what you want them to do next. Review every claim before publishing and replace generic phrases with language you actually use.

Can I use it for a company page?

Yes. Add the company context and select a professional or educational tone. For personal profiles, include first-person details to keep the post credible.

Does it create LinkedIn hashtags?

Drafts include a small number of relevant hashtags when they help discovery. Remove any that do not match your audience.

Will the post sound like me?

The more specific your source material, audience and preferred tone are, the closer the draft will be. Treat the result as an editable first draft.
