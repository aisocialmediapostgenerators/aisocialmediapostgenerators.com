Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Postcraft processes information when you use our social media writing tools. It is a product-ready template and should be reviewed for the legal entity, vendors and jurisdictions used before launch.

Information we process

We may process account details, billing status, prompts you submit, generated drafts, support messages, device and browser information, approximate location derived from IP address, and product events such as generation and copy actions. Payment card details are handled by the configured payment provider and are not stored by Postcraft.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the service, enforce usage limits, process subscriptions, troubleshoot failures, understand product performance, communicate about accounts, and comply with law. We do not sell personal information.

AI providers

Prompts may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate drafts. Do not submit confidential, regulated or personally sensitive information. Provider retention and training settings must be configured and disclosed before production launch.

Analytics and local storage

The free preview stores remaining daily generations and a limited event history in your browser. Production analytics may use a configured privacy-conscious analytics provider. The final deployment must update this section with the exact vendors and consent behavior.

Retention and security

We retain data only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described, account obligations and legal requirements. No security measure is perfect; we use reasonable technical and organizational controls appropriate to the service.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of eligible personal information, opt out of non-essential marketing, and manage browser storage. Rights vary by location.

Contact

Send privacy requests to support@aisocialmediapostgenerators.com.
